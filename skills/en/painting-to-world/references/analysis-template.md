## Recognition anchors
For each: name; center x/y normalized 0..1; occupied width/height; near/mid/far; silhouette; occlusion relationships; tolerance judgment.

## Palette and surface
Color role; approximate hex; warm/cool; material; stroke direction and scale. State uncertainty. Measurements are approximate; no fictional physical accuracy.

## Scene contract
Coordinate axes; eye height; opening camera and target; navigable surface; bounds; collisions; 4–6 destinations; close-up details; sky extent; unexplored/back-facing surfaces.

## Authored extensions
Depicted / inferred / invented, separately. Which missing spaces are filled? What does the website say about them?

## Camera route
Time / eye / target / narrative purpose. Test intermediate spline positions, not just control points.

## Visual comparison
Reference aspect ratio and viewport; actual rendered screenshot path; mismatches; repairs; remaining limitations.

## Delivery record
Build command; offline dependencies; source tree; bilingual text map; screenshots; tests actually run; untested browsers/devices; source and rights notes.
