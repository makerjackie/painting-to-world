# Artwork and assets

Vincent van Gogh, Café Terrace at Night (1888). Museum metadata: https://www.krollermuller.nl/en/collection/vincent-van-gogh-terrace-of-a-caf-at-night-place-du-forum-km-108-565

This is the successful café baseline from the MakerJackie project, with added bilingual entry points and interface localization. Meshes, material maps, labels and music are generated from code; no original painting bitmap, external model, licensed song sample or font binary is included. The imagined interior, back streets, roof space, figures and star placement are authored additions, not hidden information recovered from the painting.

The café signs are fictional, not historical evidence. No museum endorsement is claimed. Reference imagery, code, brand and reproduction rights must be assessed separately before commercial distribution. Existing package license metadata is retained; this file does not assign a new open-source license.
