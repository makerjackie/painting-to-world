# Provenance, versions and scope

## Successful baseline: Café Terrace at Night

The supplied `van-gogh-cafe-terrace-source.zip` and `cafe-terrace.html` were actually located and used. The current example preserves its renderer, scene modeling, materials and music, adding language entry points and localization for loading, accessibility and postcard text. The exact original HTML, README, QA and captures remain in `examples/cafe-terrace/archive/`. Historical checks are not counted as new tests.

Artwork: Vincent van Gogh, Terrace of a Café at Night / Café Terrace at Night, 1888. Museum reference: https://www.krollermuller.nl/en/collection/vincent-van-gogh-terrace-of-a-caf-at-night-place-du-forum-km-108-565

## Additional example: The Scream

Several screenshots of the earlier Scream world survived, but its source was not found. This edition uses their visual direction and the café's actual WebGL2 engineering to reauthor the scene, materials, figure, cameras and interface. **It is an editable rebuilt edition, not recovered original source.** Old captures remain under `archive/the-scream-previous-screenshots/` and are not evidence of the new release's rendering.

Artwork: Edvard Munch, The Scream. This example identifies the National Museum's 1893 version. Museum reference: https://www.nasjonalmuseet.no/en/collection/object/NG.M.00939

This is not a Picasso painting. The spatial design is authored and does not claim exact historical locations, physical dimensions or recovery of unseen backs. No museum painting reproduction is embedded in the current app.

## Marketing

Release folders `marketing/zh-CN/` and `marketing/en/` use captures from this edition's runnable websites; the editable HTML is the authoritative copy. ## Rights and release decisions

Treat the painting, digital reproduction, code, music, fonts and branding as separate asset categories. Historical attribution is retained; no museum endorsement is claimed. The examples' materials, models and music are generated from code. No fonts, third-party model weights, commercial songs or external music samples are distributed.
