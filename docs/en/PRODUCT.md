# Product description · Editable copy

## Product name
Painting to World · 入画 — An AI creative Skill for explorable 3D painting worlds

## One sentence
Start with a painting's composition, color and brushwork, and guide an AI in creating a world you can enter.

## Product story
A painting once kept us on one side of its frame. Now we can try sitting beside the café tables, walking around a sculptural figure, or looking across a painted neighborhood from its rooftops.

Painting to World turns the successful Café Terrace at Night workflow into a reusable Skill: study the painting, identify its most important anchors, build the space, author materials, choreograph a tour, then inspect and repair actual browser renders. A working, editable code baseline is included—not just a “generate a website” prompt.

The aim is an explorable interpretation connected to the painting's composition, mood and brushwork, rather than a generic low-poly scene or an enlarged panorama. Hidden surfaces and space beyond the frame are explicitly identified as authored extensions.

## Included

- Chinese and English Skills: full workflow, analysis template, engine interfaces, quality gates and reusable source baseline.
- Two genuine 3D examples: the successful café and a Scream implementation rebuilt for this edition, each with bilingual entry points, modular source, build scripts and test records.
- Chinese and English usage guides, invocation prompts, editing/publishing notes and this product copy.
- Release posters and editable HTML in both languages; historical AI concept posters are separately archived and labeled as non-runtime imagery.

## Uses

For independent developers learning painterly 3D, art creators building interactive stories, educators connecting paintings to spatial experience, and exploratory museum-experience concepts. It is not a professional conservation scan or a certified scholarly reconstruction tool.

## Before using

This is a creative workflow and code kit for an image-capable coding AI—not a standalone one-click image-to-3D model or hosted platform. A single image cannot determine hidden geometry. Different paintings and models may need multiple authoring passes; identical first-pass quality is not guaranteed.

Viewing requires WebGL2; building requires Node.js. The examples do not call online models at runtime, but the AI accounts and costs used during creation are separate. Model credits, cloud hosting, automatic deployment, finished video files and unlimited support are not included or promised.

Check the scope of use for the painting, digital reproduction, code and marketing assets separately. The kit does not decide your commercial license, price, support term or refund policy.
